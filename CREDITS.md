# Credits / 크레딧

창공전선(Sky Frontline)에 쓰인 외부 자료와 그 라이선스 목록이에요. 3D 모델은 전부 Sketchfab에 올라온 작품이에요. 이 판은 **팔지 않는 판**이라, 업로더가 다른 게임(War Thunder 등)에서 뽑아냈다고 적어 둔 모델도 들어 있어요. 그런 모델의 권리는 원래 게임 회사에 있고, 표의 라이선스 칸은 Sketchfab 업로더가 붙인 걸 그대로 옮긴 거예요. 그러니 이 판의 모델을 상업적으로 쓰면 안 돼요. A-5 비질란테는 **Sketchfab Free Standard** 라이선스라 아래 [라이선스 모델](#licensed-model-not-cc--라이선스-모델) 조건을 따라요. 게임 안 엔딩 크레딧에도 같은 목록이 나와요.

All 3D models come from Sketchfab uploads. This edition is **not for sale**: it includes models whose uploaders say they were extracted from other games (War Thunder and others). The rights in those models stay with the games' owners, and the licence column only repeats what each uploader declared, so do not use this edition's models commercially. The A-5 Vigilante is used under the **Sketchfab Free Standard** licence (see [Licensed model](#licensed-model-not-cc--라이선스-모델)). No endorsement by the creators is implied.

- CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ — CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Each model file also keeps its title, author, licence and source URL in `asset.extras`.
- First-person cockpits (`ck_*.glb`) are cockpit sections cut out of the credited models below and fitted around the pilot eye of each aircraft.
- Changes: every model was converted for the web (listed per model below). In the game, models may also be scaled, rotated, recoloured, split into moving parts (propellers, control surfaces, landing gear) or have parts hidden at runtime.
- Provided "as is" by the creators, without warranties.
